    How it works

    Screenshot: pick the image, or copy it and tap 📋 Paste (Ctrl/Cmd+V on a computer). The board is found automatically and shown on your screenshot as a grid; tap Use this board or drag a new box. Colours, piece shapes, walls, tracks, keys, locks, layered pieces, ice, crates and exits are read automatically. Numbers aren't read: tap each ice block, crate, lock and frozen exit and type its number. Drawing tools are under ✎ Edit board. Wrong board? Adjust brings the preview back.

    Rules: drag a piece anywhere through empty space; it leaves through an exit of its own colour that is at least as wide as the piece. Each piece that leaves counts down every ice block, crate and frozen exit by one. A piece with a key opens the lock of its own colour when it leaves. A layered piece leaves its core behind when the outer colour exits. A track (hollow outlined cell) can only be crossed by pieces of its colour — draw one with the Track tool and a colour. Crates open like ice; what's inside only shows once they do. A piece with a big ⇔/⇕ arrow only moves (and leaves) along it. ★ Star pieces can only leave through ★ star exits; normal pieces may use star exits too.

    Unknown colours (?): pieces still under ice and frozen exits. The solver plays as far as it can; do those moves, then import a new screenshot once the colours show.
