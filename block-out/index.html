🟦 Block Out Solver

Import a screenshot or draw the board, then Solve.

Drag a box over the grid of blocks: inside the frame, not including it.

Tap the board or → to play one move.

    How it works

    Screenshot: pick the image (or copy it and tap 📋 Paste / press Ctrl+V), drag a box over the blocks, Detect. Colours, piece shapes, keys, locks, ice and exits are read automatically. Numbers aren't: tap each ice block, lock and frozen exit (Select tool) and type its number.

    Rules: drag a piece anywhere through empty space; it leaves through an exit of its own colour that is at least as wide as the piece. Each piece that leaves counts down every ice block by one; every move counts down the frozen exits. A piece with a key opens the lock of its own colour when it leaves.

    Unknown colours (?): pieces still under ice and frozen exits. The solver plays as far as it can; do those moves, then import a new screenshot once the colours show.
